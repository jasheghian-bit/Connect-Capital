# CapNet Fee and Refund Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains who pays CapNet, when, and how billing disputes, corrections and refunds are handled. CapNet is operated by [FOUNDER: legal entity name].

CapNet does not hold or move funds. Funders pay merchants directly. This policy covers only CapNet's own platform fee — not any advance, payment, remittance, or fee between a merchant and a funder.

## 2. Merchants

Merchants pay CapNet no platform access fee. Because merchants pay CapNet nothing, there is nothing for CapNet to refund to a merchant.

Note that funders pay CapNet a platform fee on funded deals and may account for it when pricing their offers, so offer pricing from funders may include the platform fee.

Questions about an advance, a payment, a remittance, or a charge by a funder must be directed to that funder; CapNet cannot refund amounts it never received. You may still contact us at [FOUNDER: contact email] if you believe a funder has acted improperly on the platform.
