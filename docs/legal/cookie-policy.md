## 2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. "Similar technologies" include local storage and pixels. Some are set by us (first-party); some by service providers we use (third-party).

## 3. Categories we use

### Strictly necessary (always on)

These are required for CapNet to work and to keep accounts secure. They cannot be turned off through our banner, because the service would not function without them.

- **Authentication session** — keeps you signed in and links requests to your account. Set by our authentication provider, Clerk.
- **Security** — protects against cross-site request forgery (CSRF) and other attacks, and supports fraud and abuse detection.
- **Consent record** — remembers your cookie choices so we do not ask again on every visit.

### Preferences (optional)

Remember settings such as display preferences or dismissed notices. Used only if you allow them.

## 5. Your choices

### Cookie banner

On your first visit, our banner offers three options with equal prominence:

- **Accept all** — allows preferences and analytics.
- **Reject non-essential** — only strictly necessary cookies are used.
- **Manage** — choose category by category.

Rejecting is as easy as accepting: one click. Optional cookies are not set until you choose. You can change your choice at any time from the "Cookie settings" link in the site footer.

### Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of non-essential cookies and of any sale or sharing of personal information, for that browser. [COUNSEL: confirm GPC handling requirements by state.]

### Browser controls

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, you will not be able to sign in or use CapNet.
